# Members & roles

## Members tab

- **All**: every member, with their role, whether they are online and how much they gave to the bank. Buttons on
  the row promote ▲, demote ▼, kick ✖ or hand over leadership ♛, depending on your own role.
- **Invitations**: invite a player by name and see pending invitations.
- **Trusted**: outsiders allowed in your land with the **Ally** role (`/country trust <player>`).

The card on the right shows the **hierarchy**: each role and the heads of its members.

### Member profile

Click a member to open their profile in place of the hierarchy:

| Stat | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Status | Online now, or when they were last seen |
| Joined | How long ago they joined the country |
| Playtime | Total time played while in the country |
| Contributed | Money they put in the bank (net) |
| This week | Playtime over the last 7 days |

Below is a bar chart of their activity on each of the last 7 days (hover a bar for the exact time). Click the
member again, or the ✕, to go back to the hierarchy. Members who joined before the stats existed show *Unknown*
as their join date.

## Roles

The **Roles** tab is a table: one column per role, one row per rule. Click a cell to allow or deny.

Built-in roles:

| Role | Default |
|---|---|
| Leader | Everything, cannot be changed |
| Officer | Everything |
| Member | Use the land fully, the chest and the home teleport; manage nothing |
| Ally | Doors, using blocks, vehicles, picking up items (for trusted players and allied countries) |
| Visitor | Nothing (everyone else) |

You can create up to 6 member roles, rename them, give them a color and order them. A role can only manage roles
below it.

### Rules

**Land**: Build & break · Open containers · Use doors & gates · Use redstone · Use blocks & entities · Hurt animals ·
Harvest crops · Breed, feed & lead animals · Trade with villagers · Use boats & minecarts · Item frames & armor
stands · Use buckets · Light fires · Sleep & set spawn · Ender pearls into the land · Pick up items

**Management**: Invite players · Kick members · Claim & unclaim land · Manage roles · Edit settings · Manage markers ·
Withdraw from the bank · Use the country chest · Diplomacy & wars · Teleport home · Rent out plots · Set taxes ·
Buy research levels · Set the capital · Trust players · Capture enemy land

Each role can also have its own daily **tax** (see [Bank, taxes & research](Bank-Taxes-and-Research.md)).
