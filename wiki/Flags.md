# Flags

## Drawing your flag

**Settings → Flag** opens a pixel editor with a 48-color palette:

- left-click paints, right-click picks a color;
- *Erase* clears the flag.

The flag shows in the menus, on the map, in the ranking and on flag blocks.

## Country Flag block

Crafted with 4 wool and 3 sticks (also in the **Territoria** creative tab):

```
W W S
W W S
    S
```

- **On the ground** it stands on a pole, two blocks high, and waves in the wind.
- **Against a wall** it hangs flat, like a tapestry, and falls off if the wall is removed.

A flag block always shows the flag of the country that owns its chunk (white cloth in the wilderness). So when a
chunk is captured, its flags change by themselves.

## Banner

Hold a white banner and use `/country banner` (or the button in Settings) to turn it into a vanilla banner of
your flag.
