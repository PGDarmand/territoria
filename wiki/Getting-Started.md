# Getting started

## 1. Open the menu

Press **`N`**. The left rail switches between the hub pages:

| Icon | Page |
|---|---|
| Map | World map with every country, capital and marker |
| Flag | Your country (or the page to found / join one) |
| Crown | Ranking of every country |
| Gear (bottom) | Your personal settings (HUD, minimap, chat…) |
| Hammer (bottom, admins only) | Admin panel |

## 2. Found or join a country

Without a country, the menu shows two tabs:

- **New country**: type a name and click **Create**. You can also accept or decline invitations and join any
  country marked *Open to everyone*.
- **Rentals**: rent an apartment or plot in an existing country without joining it
  (see [Plots, rentals & embassies](Plots-Rentals-and-Embassies.md)).

The server may charge a creation cost (`creation_cost`).

## 3. Claim your first land

Open **Territory**, then left-click chunks on the map to claim them (right-click to release). A claim must be close
to you (`claim_distance`) and may cost money from the country bank.

## 4. Set your capital

On the world map (`M`), add a marker of kind **Capital** inside your land. The capital shows on everyone's map,
compass and minimap, and members can teleport to it (`/country home`, or the *Home* button in Overview).

## 5. Invite your friends

**Members → Invitations**: type a player name and click **Invite**. Give them a role in **Roles**.

That's it. Next: [Countries](Countries.md).
