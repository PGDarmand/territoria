# Countries

A country has a name, a color, a flag, a description, members, land, a bank, a shared chest and a research level.

## The country menu

The tabs are grouped:

| Group | Tabs |
|---|---|
| People | **Overview**, **Members** |
| Growth | **Bank**, **Research** |
| World | **Diplomacy**, **Territory**, **Rentals** |
| Organisation | **Roles**, **Settings** |

The status bar at the bottom always shows the vital signs: claims, members, bank, level and power.

## Overview

Your country at a glance: flag, description, key numbers, the latest events and the **Home** teleport button.

## Settings

Members with the *Edit settings* rule can change:

- the **color** (used for the land on maps, the name tag and the menu accent);
- the **flag** (see [Flags](Flags.md));
- the **description** and other texts;
- the land **flags**:

| Flag | Effect |
|---|---|
| PvP | Players can fight inside your land |
| Explosions | TNT and creepers can break blocks in your land |
| Fire spread | Fire can spread inside your land |
| Open to everyone | Anyone can join without an invitation |
| Recruiting | Shown as recruiting in lists |

## Name tags, chat and TAB

The country name shows before member names above heads, in the chat and in the TAB list. Members can talk privately:

- `/cc <message>` sends one message to the country;
- the **Country chat** toggle (personal settings) or `/country chat` sends all your messages to the country.

A personal **sidebar** with the country info can be turned on in the settings (or `/country sidebar`).

## Leaving and disbanding

- Any member can leave (Settings, or `/country leave`).
- The leader can **disband** the country. This deletes it and frees all its land.
- The leader can hand over leadership from **Members** (crown button).

## Ranking

The **Ranking** page sorts every country by **Worth**, **Claims**, **Members**, **Bank**, **Power**, **Level** or
**Age**. Hover a column title to see what it counts. *Worth* adds the bank, the money in the country chest, the
research bought and the value of the land.
