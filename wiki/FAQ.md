# FAQ

**Do players need the mod?**
Yes. Territoria must be installed on the server and on every client (same version). A player without it gets a
message explaining what to install.

**Does it work in singleplayer?**
Yes. The integrated server runs everything; you are the admin.

**Which economy does it use?**
Emeralds by default. With `economy: auto` it picks a mod economy, Vault or a command bridge when one is
available. See [Permissions & compatibility](Permissions-and-Compatibility.md).

**Can a new player compete with old countries?**
Yes, if you want it to. Power (`claims_per_active`) ties land to *active* members, upkeep makes huge empty land
costly, and players without a country can still rent a plot.

**Someone griefed my land. Who did it?**
Without the *Build & break* rule, outsiders can't change anything. Check your role rules, trusted players and
plot renters. Admins can read `/territoriaadmin logs`.

**How do I reset a country's money or level?**
Admin panel → Moderation, or `/territoriaadmin setbalance` / `setlevel`.

**Where is the data?**
In the world folder (`<world>/data/`). Back up the world and you back up Territoria.

**Can I change the icons?**
Yes, with a resource pack: see [Permissions & compatibility](Permissions-and-Compatibility.md#resource-packs).
